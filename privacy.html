Privacy policy

Last updated: 23 November 2026

What ReelRecall accesses

When you connect Google Drive, ReelRecall asks Google for read-only access to your Drive (the drive.readonly scope) and your email address. It uses this to list the folders you choose and to read the video files in them.

How the data is used

ReelRecall downloads or streams those videos to build a searchable index (transcripts, scene descriptions and thumbnails) and to play them back. All of this happens on your own computer. The index, the sign-in tokens and any copies stay there; sign-in tokens are stored encrypted.

Sharing

ReelRecall has no server of its own. It does not send your Google data to the developer or to anyone else, does not sell it, and does not use it for advertising. Data from Google APIs is not used to develop, improve or train generalized AI or machine-learning models.

ReelRecall's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Removing access

Use “Sign out” in ReelRecall, which also revokes its access with Google, or remove it at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting ReelRecall's data folder removes everything it stored.
