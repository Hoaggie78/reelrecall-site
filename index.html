ReelRecall

ReelRecall is a personal desktop app that catalogs a video library into searchable moments. It runs on the owner's own computer.

It can read videos from cloud storage the owner connects, including Google Drive. Google Drive access is read-only: ReelRecall lists and downloads videos from the folders the owner chooses, and never changes, uploads or deletes anything there.
